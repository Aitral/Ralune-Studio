## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Narrative
The brand operates at the intersection of architectural poise, editorial warmth, and digital engineering. It embodies the concept of "The Threshold of Ideas" (*El umbral de las ideas*)—a conceptual architectural passage where nascent creative sparks traverse discovery, design, and structural execution into tangible business realities. 

Its voice is cultured, serene, humanistic, and precise. It rejects tech-bro hyperbole, artificial dashboards, metric braggadocio, and gratuitous 3D mascots in favor of quiet confidence, tactile restraint, and editorial authority.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Discerning founders, design leaders, cultural entrepreneurs, and progressive enterprises looking for human-centered digital experiences without aesthetic commoditization.
- **Emotional Response:** An immediate sense of deliberate pacing, atmospheric clarity, spatial depth, and intellectual trust. The user should feel they have stepped inside an architectural atelier rather than a tech startup landing page.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Expression
- **Contemporary Editorial Craft with Contextual Glassmorphism:** A tailored fusion of classical editorial composition and modern structural fluidity. Broad panoramic canvases meet disciplined proportions, warm organic neutrals, botanical greens, refined serif headlines, and functional sans-serif utility.
- **Tactile Materiality:** Translucent frosted glass layers (contextual backdrops with 12px blur), ivory architectural stone surfaces, and pill-shaped structural controls. Surface transitions are grounded in natural light dynamics and spatial depth rather than synthetic borders or synthetic neon glows.

## Colors

### Color Philosophy & Role Assignment
The palette builds depth through chromatic tension between warm organic light minerals (Cream, Sand, Ivory) and deep botanical atmospheres (Forest, Sage, Emerald). 

- **Primary (`#1F5A45` in Light / `#0F3D2E` in Dark):** The grounding tonal weight. In light mode, Forest serves as high-contrast typographic ink, focal architectural containers, and navigation brand marks. In dark mode, it descends into an immersive, deep canopy background.
- **Secondary (`#23C16B` in Light / `#10C16B` in Dark):** The kinetic spark. Emerald is reserved strictly for primary interactive confirmations, focal badges, dynamic progress rings, and purposeful hover affordances. It is never applied indiscriminately across expansive surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#9CCFBB` in Light / `#557A6B` in Dark):** Sage provides contextual transitions, pill-badge backdrops, and muted surface fills.
- **Neutral (`#F3F0E8` in Light / `#1B1F1E` in Dark):** The spatial foundation. Light mode rests on warm Cream parchment; dark mode anchors upon deep Charcoal panels with Ivory text.

### Light & Dark Mode Specifications

#### Light Mode (Editorial Canvas)
- **Canvas Base:** `#F3F0E8` (Cream)
- **Primary Text & Headings:** `#1F5A45` (Forest)
- **Secondary / Supporting Body Text:** `#1F5A45` at 78% opacity (`rgba(31, 90, 69, 0.78)`)
- **Muted Notes & Metadata:** `#1F5A45` at 56% opacity (`rgba(31, 90, 69, 0.56)`)
- **Interactive Accents:** `#23C16B` (Emerald)
- **Dividers & Structural Rules:** `#B7B8B0` (Stone) at 40% opacity

#### Dark Mode (Subterranean Botanical)
- **Canvas Base:** `#0F3D2E` (Deep Forest)
- **Recessed Panels & Overlays:** `#1B1F1E` (Charcoal)

## Typography

### Structural Pairings & Hierarchy
The typographic architecture creates an intentional tension between **Fraunces** (an expressive, optical serif imbued with editorial gravitas and historical warmth) and **Inter** (an unembellished, highly legible neo-grotesque for frictionless technical delivery).

- **Fraunces Regular 400:** Reserved for monumental narrative anchors—the primary hero display, section headers, architectural portal inscriptions, and overlay navigation anchors. It possesses organic variable optical weight without harsh industrial stiffness.
- **Fraunces Italic 400:** Applied strictly to expressive editorial nuances, specifically the rotating word capsule within the hero, short philosophical sub-lines, and project categorization accents.
- **Inter (400, 500, 600):** Governs structural usability: body paragraphs, form inputs, metadata chips, legal colophons, navigation links, and compact interface controls.

### Implementation Rules
- **No Monospace Voice:** The studio identity expresses technical mastery through refined typographic proportion, not terminal-style coding stereotypes.
- **No Text Gradients:** Text color remains flat, resolute, and accessible across all surfaces to preserve high typographic legibility.
- **Fixed Width Container for Dynamic Hero Words:** The rotating word within the hero capsule (`realidades`, `experiencias`, `conexiones`) must render within a container configured with a predetermined width matching the longest word string (`experiencias`), preventing horizontal reflow or vertical layout shift during transitions.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture & Geometry
The layout system balances disciplined structure with expansive editorial rhythm:
- **Maximum Content Canvas:** 1280px, centered within a reference viewport of 1440px.
- **Grid Composition:** 12-column dynamic fluid grid on desktop (`1440px`), 8-column layout on tablet (`768px-1023px`), and 4-column column stack on mobile (`390px`).
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence:** Major thematic chapters are separated by generous breathing room: 80px–128px on desktop and 56px–80px on mobile viewports.

### Breakpoint Strategy
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Full architectural panoramic grid. Horizontal continuous disciplines band with hover-pause trigger. Asymmetrical 3-up project portfolio (1 featured full-scale piece spanning 8 columns, 2 stacked companion explorations spanning 4 columns).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Fluid margin of 40px (`2.5rem`). Asymmetric portfolio shifts to a sequential 2+1 grid.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** Compact fluid canvas with strict 24px (`1.5rem`) side margins. Horizontal marquee transforms into a clean, wrapped static typographic lockup to avoid performance and accessibility friction. Forms and interactive targets maintain a minimum vertical height of 48px with 16px minimum text size to prevent automatic iOS zoom.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Layers & Visual Stratification
Depth is communicated through architectural stratification, light permeability, and optical backdrop filters rather than heavy drop shadows.

1. **Base Surface (Ground Plane):** Flat `#F3F0E8` (Cream in light mode) or `#0F3D2E` (Forest in dark mode). Provides total grounding with zero shadow or elevation.
2. **Contextual Glass (Floating Nav & Portal Captions):**
   - Light Mode: `rgba(243, 240, 232, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(140%)` and a subtle 1px border of `rgba(183, 184, 176, 0.35)`.
   - Dark Mode: `rgba(15, 61, 46, 0.80)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(140%)` and a 1px border of `rgba(107, 115, 112, 0.3)`.
   - Shadow: `0 8px 32px -8px rgba(27, 31, 30, 0.06)`.
3. **Elevated Panels & Case Studies:**
   - Layered surfaces sit on discrete 1px micro-borders (`#B7B8B0` at 30% alpha) accompanied by ambient shadows: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(27, 31, 30, 0.05)`.
4. **Overlay Menu Modal:**
   - Full-bleed Forest surface (`#0F3D2E`) sitting at top z-index with a 24px border radius on inner containment cards, utilizing soft ambient outer falloff: `0 32px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Contour Discipline
The design system operates with a dual shape hierarchy: **Architectural Structural Containers** and **Organic Pill Capsules**.

- **Cards, Panels, Modal Frames, and Media Framing:** Standardized at `rounded-lg` (12px to 16px / `0.75rem` to `1rem`). This curvature balances modern industrial precision with natural stone geometry, preventing cold utilitarian sharpness.
- **Interactive Controls, Form Buttons, Badges, and Hero Word Containers:** Configured as pure capsules (`rounded-full` / `9999px`). They invite immediate tactile touch, contrasting against expansive rectangular photographic frames.
- **Architectural Portal Apertures:** Circular geometry and sweeping arches inspired by monolithic stone portals, framing interior content with soft, mathematically balanced contours.

## Components

### 1. Interactive Buttons
- **Primary Pill Action:**
  - Background: Forest (`#1F5A45`) in light mode; Emerald (`#10C16B`) in dark mode.
  - Text: Ivory (`#EDE6D9`) in light mode; Charcoal (`#1B1F1E`) in dark mode.
  - Typography: `Inter Medium 15px` (`label-md`).
  - Height: 48px desktop / 48px mobile. Padding: 12px 28px. Radius: `9999px`.
  - State Transitions: Hover elevates slightly with a micro-scale of `1.02` and subtle brightness shift; active tap registers scale `0.97` for 120ms (`cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Pill:**
  - Border: 1px solid Forest (`#1F5A45`) at 25% opacity.
  - Background: Transparent (or contextual glass `rgba(243, 240, 232, 0.6)` on media).
  - Hover: Border opacity increases to 100%, background shifts to Sage (`#9CCFBB` at 15%).

### 2. The Hero Variable Word Capsule
- Structural pill housing rotating conceptual words (`realidades`, `experiencias`, `conexiones`).
- Background: Forest (`#1F5A45`), Text: Fraunces Italic (`#EDE6D9`).
- Vertical padding 4px, horizontal padding 20px, height matched to line-height box.
- Transition: Opacity + vertical translation (`translateY(8px)` to `0px`) over 240ms. Minimum static interval duration: 3.6s. Text change is silent to screen readers via `aria-hidden` on duplicates with a stable offscreen accessible string.

### 3. Asymmetric Project Exploration Cards
- Cards feature three distinct conceptual pieces: *Forma / Galería digital*, *Senda / Historias en movimiento*, and *Umbral / Nuevos mundos*.
- Surface layout: Visual asset occupies 82% of card volume; metadata sits directly below the asset (never obscured inside the image).
- Badges: Pill chip with 12px `Inter SemiBold`, reading strictly "Exploración conceptual".
- Hover Micro-interaction: Image container scales to `1.02` over 200ms without displacing the outer frame; adjacent indicator arrow shifts horizontally +3px.

### 4. Process Rows (The Studio)
- Replaces generic cloned card grids with three editorial process lines: *01 Entender*, *02 Diseñar*, *03 Construir*.
- Border top: 1px continuous hairline (`#B7B8B0` at 35%).
- Grid: Step number in Fraunces, bold title in Fraunces 28px, followed by an Inter 16px explanatory thesis.
- Hover State: Background tint glows with subtle Sage at 8% opacity.

### 5. Input Fields & Textareas
- Labels: Permanent top-aligned `Inter Medium 13px` (`label-sm`), never relying solely on disappearing placeholders.
- Input Height: 52px (Textarea: 130px minimum).
- Field Background: Sand (`#DCCFB8` at 25%) in light mode; Charcoal (`#1B1F1E`) in dark mode.
- Borders: 1px solid Stone (`#B7B8B0` at 50%).
- Focus State: Visible, high-contrast 2px ring in Emerald (`#23C16B`) with zero layout shift.
- Prototype Notification: Form submission initiates client validation and triggers an inline informational banner: *"Esto es una demostración. Tu mensaje no se ha enviado."*

### 6. Architectural Portal Element
- Visual centerpiece depicting monolithic stone architecture with an organic circular aperture.
- Scroll interaction: Settles from scale 1.04 down to 1.00 smoothly over 650ms once entering screen view.
- Overlaid glass bar on lower third containing twin micro-inscriptions: *"Más ideas. Mundos reales."* and *"Creatividad humana. Precisión digital."*
